FAQ

What is my IP?

Your IP address is your public identifier on the internet and can reveal your location and ISP.

What is a DNS leak?

A DNS leak exposes your DNS requests even when using a VPN, revealing your real location or ISP.

What is a WebRTC leak?

WebRTC can reveal your real IP address through browser technology, even when using a VPN.

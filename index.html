Frequently Asked Questions

What is a WebRTC data leak?

WebRTC allows browsers to create direct peer-to-peer connections for streaming. However, this protocol can occasionally expose your actual residential public and local IP addresses, completely bypassing an active proxy server or standard VPN tunnel configuration.

How does browser fingerprint tracking function?

Websites utilize sophisticated Canvas and hardware query methods to compile unique identity sets from your specific computer configuration. This identity calculation bypasses browser cookie deletions to track your online sessions persistently.

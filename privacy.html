Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

At Privacy Shield Audit, accessible from netprivacycheck.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that are collected and recorded by Privacy Shield Audit and how we use it.

1. Local Browser Diagnostics (Zero Logs)

Privacy Shield Audit is a client-side diagnostic tool. All privacy tests—including the WebRTC leak test, device info queries, and canvas fingerprint tracking calculations—are executed directly inside your local web browser. We do not transmit, collect, or store any of your network or identity diagnostic data on any server.

2. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to netprivacycheck.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://google.com

3. Our Advertising Partners

Some of advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include:

  • Google AdSense: Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data. For easier access, we hyperlinked to their Privacy Policies above.

4. Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Privacy Shield Audit, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Privacy Shield Audit has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

Privacy Shield Audit's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

6. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

We fully support global data protection rights. Because our diagnostic tool processes your network parameters entirely on the client-side layer and logs zero private databases, we do not store, hold, or sell any personal data belonging to European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA) residents.
